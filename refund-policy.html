Customer policy

Refund &
Cancellation.

Last updated: 25 September 2026. This policy explains refunds, cancellations and disputes for custom digital services supplied by jackdevsss.

ScopeCancellationRefundsRevisionsDeliveryDisputesConsumers

1. Scope

Services are custom digital development services, including Pavlov VR maps, custom Pavlov mods, Unreal Engine Blueprint systems, integration, optimization and related modding work. The agreed quote or written project brief defines the specific deliverables, price and estimated timeline.

2. Cancellation

You may request cancellation by email or Discord. If work has not started, amounts paid for work not yet performed will normally be refunded, except any clearly agreed non-refundable third-party cost already incurred for your project.

Once work has started, any refund is assessed against work already completed, reserved production time, delivered milestones and non-recoverable project costs. No new charges will be made after an accepted cancellation unless they relate to amounts already due under the agreed scope.

3. Refunds

If jackdevsss cannot provide the agreed service, a full or proportionate refund will be offered for the affected undelivered work. If a delivered item materially fails to match the written agreed scope, please report it promptly so the issue can first be corrected where reasonably possible.

Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method through Stripe. Bank/card processing times are controlled by the relevant payment provider and financial institution.

4. Revisions and scope changes

Unless a quote states otherwise, one small-medium update is included in a commissioned project. Whether a requested update falls within that allowance is determined reasonably at the time based on its scope and the work required. Additional updates, new features, major redesigns or requests outside the original scope are chargeable and will be quoted before implementation. Custom Pavlov mods start from $50 USD. Changes to commissioned work are quoted separately according to scope.

5. Delivery and delays

Delivery dates are estimates unless a specific guaranteed deadline is expressly agreed in writing. If a material delay occurs, the customer will be informed and an updated delivery estimate will be provided. Express delivery starts from $50 USD for maps and $25 USD for mods. Typical express estimates are 9-12 days for maps (from $50 USD) and 5-9 days for mods (from $25 USD), depending on complexity and availability. These estimates include development and testing.

6. Payment disputes

Please contact jackdevsss before opening a payment dispute so the issue can be reviewed and, where appropriate, corrected or refunded. Nothing in this policy limits rights that cannot legally be excluded.

7. EU/Italian consumer rights

Where mandatory consumer law applies, consumers may have a statutory right of withdrawal for distance contracts. For services, the withdrawal period generally runs from conclusion of the contract. If you expressly request performance to begin during that period, applicable law may require payment proportionate to the service already performed; if the service has been fully performed after the required express request and acknowledgements, the statutory withdrawal right may cease where the law permits.

This contractual policy does not replace or reduce mandatory rights under applicable Italian or EU consumer law.

8. Contact

Refund or cancellation requests: info.jackdevss@gmail.com or Discord @jackdevsss.
