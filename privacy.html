1. Data collected

This static website does not include a registration system or advertising tracker. If you contact jackdevsss, information you choose to provide may include your email address or Discord account, project requirements, correspondence and billing information necessary to manage a commission.

Data may be used to answer enquiries, prepare quotes, perform and administer commissioned work, provide support, keep necessary transaction records, prevent abuse and comply with legal obligations. 3. Stripe payments

Payments are processed by Stripe. Payment-card information is handled through Stripe's systems rather than stored by this static website. Stripe processes personal data under its own privacy terms and applicable data-protection obligations.

The site may be hosted through GitHub Pages or another hosting provider, which may process technical request data such as IP addresses and server logs. Links to Discord, Stripe and other third-party services are governed by those providers' own privacy practices.

Enquiry data is kept only as reasonably necessary to handle the request. Contract, payment and accounting-related records may be retained for periods required by applicable law or necessary to establish, exercise or defend legal claims.

6. Your rights

Where the GDPR applies, you may have rights including access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection and data portability where applicable, as well as the right to lodge a complaint with the competent supervisory authority.
